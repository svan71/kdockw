A dock for Plasma 6 on Wayland

KDockW brings parabolic zoom, grouped apps, and live previews of open and minimized windows to your KDE desktop.

Made for Plasma on Wayland

  • Magnify dock icons with smooth parabolic zoom.
  • Group application windows, preview open and minimized windows, and switch between them from the dock.
  • Pin desktop launchers, reorder items, and drop files onto applications.
  • Use KDE-native settings, keyboard navigation, and Russian localization.

Packages are available for Debian 13, Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, and Fedora 45 from the latest release.

Origins and license

KDockW is an independently developed spiritual successor to Latte Dock. It’s based on Krema, originally created by Byeonghoon Yoo, and preserves the project’s Git history and copyright notices. KDockW is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.
